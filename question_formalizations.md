# Question Formalizations: Homework 6

## Task 1: Noise

* **Version 1 (Stakeholder Question):** 
  "The mayor wants to know if noise issues tend to stem from different causes across the year."
* **Version 2 (First Refinement - More Measurable):** 
  "Are different types of noise complaints (such as residential, commercial, or street noise) more frequent during certain months of the year?"
* **Version 3 (Second Refinement - Mapping to Available Data):** 
  "How do the monthly proportions and absolute volumes of specific 311 noise complaint categories and descriptors shift across the twelve months of 2024?"
* **Version 4 (Final Quantifiable Version):** 
  "What are the relative monthly frequencies of individual 311 noise complaint descriptor categories in 2024, and which specific noise types exhibit statistically significant volume distribution shifts across seasonal periods?"
