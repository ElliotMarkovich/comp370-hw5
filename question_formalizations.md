# Question Formalizations: Homework 6

## Task 1: Noise

* **Version 1 (Stakeholder Question):** 
  "The mayor wants to know if noise issues tend to stem from different causes across the year."
* **Version 2 (First Refinement - More Measurable):** 
  "Are different types of noise complaints (such as residential, commercial, or street noise) more frequent during certain months of the year?"
* **Version 3 (Second Refinement - Mapping to Available Data):** 
  "How do the monthly proportions and absolute volumes of specific 311 noise complaint categories and descriptors shift across the twelve months of 2024?"
* **Version 4 (Final Quantifiable Version):** 
  "What are the relative monthly frequencies of individual 311 noise complaint descriptor categories in 2024, and which specific noise types exhibit statistically significant volume distribution shifts across seasonal periods?"

  ## Task 2: Urban Rodents

* **Version 1 (Stakeholder Question):** 
  "The Departments of Sanitation and Health want to know where in the city rats and mice are most likely to create sanitation issues, focusing on building types and property structures rather than geographic locations."
* **Version 2 (First Refinement - More Measurable):** 
  "Which specific 311 location types (such as multi-family homes, commercial buildings, or mixed-use properties) account for the highest volume of rodent and rat-related complaints in 2024?"
* **Version 3 (Second Refinement - Mapping to Available Data):** 
  "How do complaint volumes and proportions for rodent-related categories (e.g., 'Rodent', 'Mouse', 'Rat') map across the distinct `Location Type` and property descriptor attributes recorded in the 2024 311 dataset?"
* **Version 4 (Final Quantifiable Version):** 
  "What are the relative concentrations of rat and mice complaint volumes across various structural property and location types in NYC for 2024, and which building categories exhibit disproportionately high densities of rodent-driven sanitation issues?"
